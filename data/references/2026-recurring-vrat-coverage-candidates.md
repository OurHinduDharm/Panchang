Snapshot: 2026-10-30. Compared the live `data/festivals/2026-MM.json` month files with the 2026 recurring observance index published by Tathaastu and the 2026 festival/vrat list from Shubhdin Panchang.

This is a candidate queue, not a set of approved additions. Tathaastu's page does not state a selected city in the captured page, while Shubhdin explicitly says its listed dates are for New Delhi. OHD uses Pithoragarh as its default observer. A date difference is therefore not automatically an error; every candidate must be recalculated for Pithoragarh and resolved using the relevant vrata decision time.

Additional Purnima/Amavasya candidate review is needed where a monthly tithi is not represented as its own event, or where it is combined with a festival title. Combined entries may already satisfy visitor coverage; do not add duplicate cards merely to create a separate monthly label.

## Required verification before any addition

For every candidate:
1. Recalculate the tithi interval for the Pithoragarh observer and IST.
2. Apply the observance-specific decision point: moonrise for Sankashti; sunrise/Arunodaya and Smarta/Vaishnava distinction for Ekadashi; Pradosh for Pradosh; Nishita for Masik Shivaratri; the applicable vrata rule for Durgashtami, Vinayaka Chaturthi and Skanda Shashthi.
3. Check whether the same observance is already present under an alternate name, festival title or combined record.
4. Confirm Hindu month/paksha and weekday; keep the Purnimanta month convention consistent.
5. Confirm article link and description. Do not create a false muhurta link or promise a time calculation that is not implemented.
6. Keep disputed dates as needs-review; do not let one calendar's date list auto-approve a date when the observance is location- or tradition-sensitive.
