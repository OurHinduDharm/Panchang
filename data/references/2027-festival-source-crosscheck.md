# 2027 Festival Source Cross-check — Completed date review

Review date: 2026-11-02
Scope: all twelve `data/festivals/2027-*.json` files; 121 event records.

## Calendar comparison

The 2027 monthly catalog was compared with Drik Panchang's annual Hindu calendar, Drik's dedicated festival pages, and Global Panchang's Dehradun calendar. All 121 event records are marked approved and all twelve month-level files are approved. The source/location metadata now reflects the completed calendar-date review.

## Key decisions and corrections

- Krishna Janmashtami: retain 24 August as the Smarta observance. The later Vaishnava date is not added as a separate OHD card.
- Akshaya Tritiya: retain 9 May, matching Drik's Rishikesh and Haridwar pages. Global Panchang Dehradun lists 8 May; this is retained as a documented location/source difference, not silently forced into OHD.
- Parashurama Jayanti: 8 May, separate from Akshaya Tritiya on 9 May, following the Pradosha-based rule on Drik's dedicated page.
- Makar Sankranti: 15 January; the solar ingress occurs 14 January at 21:14, while the local punya-kala/observance is on 15 January.
- Maha Shivaratri: 6 March, checked against Drik Pithoragarh.
- Diwali / Lakshmi Puja: 29 October, checked against Drik Pithoragarh.
- Samaveda Upakarma: 3 September, checked against Drik day Panchang (Hasta) and independent Samaveda Upakarma calendars.
- Durga Puja begins 5 October in Drik's dedicated calendar; this is distinct from Vijayadashami on 9 October.
- 18 June Jyeshtha Purnima / Vat Purnima card has no Kabir Jayanti alias, following the user's earlier removal decision.

## Chaturmas convention

Devshayani Ekadashi 14 July and Smarta Devutthana Ekadashi 10 November are the OHD dates. OHD does not introduce Vaishnava-only Chaturmas dates. The completion and card-visibility dates remain governed by the separately approved Chaturmas rules.
