# 2029–2030 Festival Source Cross-check — Completed date review

Review date: 2026-11-02

## 2029

The 2029 monthly calendar was compared with Drik Panchang's annual Hindu calendar and dedicated festival pages. The following omissions were added:
- 25 June: Vat Purnima Vrat.
- 22 August: Onam / Thiruvonam.
- 24 August: Varalakshmi Vrat and Gayatri Jayanti (one combined card).
- 13 September: Balarama Jayanti.
- 20 December: Dattatreya Jayanti added to the Margashirsha Purnima card.
- 21 December: total lunar eclipse visible in Pithoragarh, added with local phase timings.

Solar eclipses on 14 January, 12 June, 11 July and 5 December 2029 are not locally visible in Pithoragarh. The 26 June total lunar eclipse is also not in the local Pithoragarh visibility list; no local cards were added for those events.

## 2030

The 2030 monthly dates were compared against Drik Panchang's annual Hindu calendar. The core annual festival dates align with the OHD catalog. The following locally visible lunar eclipse was added:
- 15–16 June: partial lunar eclipse visible in Pithoragarh; partial phase 10:51 PM to 1:15 AM, maximum at 12:03 AM, and penumbral phase ends at 2:22 AM.
- 9–10 December 2030 is only a penumbral lunar eclipse (negative umbral magnitude). It is not added as a Hindu grahan card.
- The 1 June 2030 annular solar eclipse is visible as a partial eclipse in Pithoragarh. The public Vrat-Parv card retains the date and visibility only; exact local contacts are withheld because the captured city-table times conflict with independent global eclipse timing.
- Audit correction (14 Nov 2026): removed the unsupported 3:57 PM / 5:15:56 PM / 6:38:21 PM values from `data/festivals/2030-06.json`; retained the event date and added a regression assertion so those local times cannot silently return.
- The 15–16 June lunar eclipse description expresses maximum eclipse as 16 June 00:03 IST to make the midnight date unambiguous.
