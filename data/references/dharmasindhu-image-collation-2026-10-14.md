## 2. Visually confirmed passage readings

### A. Pāraṇa paragraph (Khemraj printed p. 31, image 0054)

The image visibly reads, with punctuation normalized and line breaks removed:

> कलामात्राया अपि द्वादश्याः अभावे त्रयोदश्यां पारणम्। द्वादश्या मध्याह्नोर्ध्वं सत्त्वे प्रातर्मुहूर्तत्रयमध्य एव पारणं न मध्याह्नादूर्ध्व इति बहवः। बहूनां कर्मकालानां बाधापत्तेरपराह्ण एव इति केचित्॥

The Hindi commentary directly below explains the two views separately. Therefore:
- The **बहवः** and **केचित्** alternatives are real textual alternatives in this witness; do not merge them into one universal rule.
- The “three morning muhūrtas” anchor and the apparent tension between the clause's condition and its prescribed time still require a rule-context decision before algorithmic conversion.
- The Trayodaśī fallback is present in the text, but its full antecedent/context must be preserved. It is not a universal fallback for every Ekādaśī.

### B. Śravaṇa-Dvādaśī (Khemraj printed pp. 31–32, images 0054–0055)

The relevant Sanskrit is visually legible as:

> द्वादश्यां सर्वमासेषु शुक्लायां कृष्णायां वा श्रवणयोगे शक्तेनैकादशीद्वादश्योर्द्वयोरुपवासः कार्यः। अशक्तेनैकादश्यां फलाहारानुकल्पं कृत्वा श्रवणद्वादश्यामुपवासः कार्यः। विष्णुशृङ्खलयोगसत्त्वे त्वेकादश्यामेव श्रवणद्वादशीप्रयुक्तमप्युपवासं कृत्वा द्वादश्यां श्रवणयोगरहितायां पारणं कार्यम्। द्वादश्याः श्रवणतो न्यूनत्वे श्रवणयुक्तायामपि द्वादश्यामेव पारणम्। द्वादश्युल्लङ्घने दोषात्। विष्णुशृङ्खलयोगादिनिर्णयो भाद्रपदमासगतश्रवणद्वादशीप्रकरणे वक्ष्यते॥

Orthographic details such as anusvāra/visarga and conjunct spelling should be checked against the image when quoting in a formal edition. The text itself points to a later Bhādrapada Śravaṇa-Dvādaśī section for the Viṣṇu-śṛṅkhala decision; that cross-reference must be researched before implementing the yoga predicate.

### C. Eight Mahādvādaśīs (Khemraj printed p. 32, image 0055; Hindi explanation continues on p. 33)

The Sanskrit names and pairings are visually legible:

> अथाष्टौ महाद्वादश्यः। शुद्धाधिकैकादशीयुक्ता द्वादशी उन्मीलनीसंज्ञा। द्वादश्येव शुद्धाधिका वर्धते सा वञ्जुली। सूर्योदये एकादशी ततः क्षयगामिनी द्वादशी द्वितीयसूर्योदये त्रयोदशी एवमहोरात्रे तिथित्रयस्पर्शात् त्रिस्पर्शा संज्ञा द्वादशी। दर्शस्य पौर्णमास्या वा यदा दिनवृद्धिस्तदा पक्षवर्धिनी संज्ञा। पुष्क्षर्क्षयुता जया। श्रवणयुता विजया। पुनर्वसयुता जयन्ती। रोहिणीयुता पापनाशिनी। एताः पापक्षयमुक्तिकाम उपवसेत्। श्रवणयुता त्वेकादशीवन्नित्या॥

The first nakṣatra compound appears as **पुष्यर्क्षयुता** in the scan; normalize to Puṣya-yukta only as a reading aid, not as a claim that the original has been critically edited. The third definition explicitly uses the idea of *tithi-traya-sparśa* (“three-tithi contact”), clarifying the earlier OCR-corrupted compound. “दिनवृद्धि” is indeed the printed phrase in the Pakṣavardhinī definition, but the operational meaning of that term is not defined on this line.

### D. Additional observance and pāraṇa notes (Khemraj p. 33, image 0056; Rajvaidya p. 32, image 0056 and p. 33, image 0057)

The Khemraj Hindi commentary states that:
- if the relevant Ekādaśī/Dvādaśī observances coincide, the text says to fast by *tantra*; if separate, a capable person may undertake two fasts;
- the Śravaṇa-nakṣatra conjunction is acceptable even for a muhūrta मात्र;
- Puṣya and the other named nakṣatra conjunctions are described as extending from sunrise to sunset;
- the general pāraṇa statement refers to the tithi–nakṣatra conjunction ending, with wording that appears to distinguish the end of both from the end of either one.

The final Sanskrit sentence appears to read approximately:

> पारणं तु तिथिनक्षत्रसंयोगोपरमेणोभयान्तेऽन्यतरान्ते च इति सर्वसामान्यनिर्णयः।

**Important:** the sandhi/segmentation around “उभयान्तेऽन्यतरान्ते च” needs a second close reading before choosing an algorithm. Do not translate it automatically as “whichever ends first.” Preserve it as a source-backed but semantically unresolved timing instruction.
