## B. Hindi meaning supplied by the project owner

- If not even a kalā of Dvādaśī is available, pāraṇa is stated to occur on Trayodaśī; its exact contextual scope still needs checking.
- When Dvādaśī is present after midday, one view attributed to **बहवः** says pāraṇa should be within the first three morning muhūrtas, not after midday. Another view attributed to **केचित्** prefers the afternoon because other ritual timings would be obstructed. Preserve both views separately.
- Eight Mahādvādaśīs as supplied:
  1. **Unmīlanī** — Dvādaśī joined with pure/extended Ekādaśī (exact technical meaning to verify).
  2. **Vañjulī** — Dvādaśī itself is pure/extended and increases (exact technical meaning to verify).
  3. **Trispr̥śā** — Ekādaśī at one sunrise, then a waning/kṣaya Dvādaśī, and Trayodaśī at the second sunrise; three tithis touch one ahorātra (exact Sanskrit compound/sequence to verify).
  4. **Pakṣavardhinī** — day-increase condition on Amāvāsyā or Pūrṇimā (definition of “day increase” to verify).
  5. **Jayā** — Dvādaśī joined with Puṣya.
  6. **Vijayā** — Dvādaśī joined with Śravaṇa.
  7. **Jayantī** — Dvādaśī joined with Punarvasu.
  8. **Pāpanāśinī** — Dvādaśī joined with Rohiṇī.
- The supplied translation says these are observed for sin-destruction / merit; Śravaṇa-yukta Dvādaśī is described as nitya like Ekādaśī.
- Additional notes supplied: when two Ekādaśī fasts fall on one day, use a tantra method; if they fall on separate days, a capable observer performs both; if both vows were undertaken but both cannot be completed, Dvādaśī fast is said to confer the merit of both. These are ritual/capacity notes, not astronomical date-selection predicates.
- The translation suggests even one muhūrta of Śravaṇa conjunction may count, while Pushya and the other named nakṣatras require a sunrise-to-sunset conjunction. The exact Sanskrit scope must be verified.
- The translation mentions pāraṇa after the end of the tithi–nakṣatra conjunction or after the end of one of them. The Sanskrit syntax must be checked before deciding what this means algorithmically.
