## Useful cross-checks

- [Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, Vilāsa 13, verses 267–268](https://anantasatsang.org/scripture/radha-bhakti/hari-bhakti-vilasa/vilasa-13) provides verse-level Sanskrit for Unmīlanī and Vañjulī and is useful for locating duration/extension terminology. It is a separate textual witness; do not silently substitute it for Dharmasindhu p. 17.
- [Drik Panchang — Vañjulī Mahādvādaśī](https://www.drikpanchang.com/dwadashi/mahadwadashi/vanjuli-mahadwadashi.html) describes Dvādaśī extension and sunrise-to-sunset presence; use as a modern explanatory cross-check, not primary proof.
- [Drik Panchang — Hindi Mahādvādaśī summaries](https://www.drikpanchang.com/dwadashi/mahadwadashi/vanjuli-mahadwadashi.html?lang=hi) gives concise definitions for the named observances; use for discovery only.
- [G. A. Grierson, alphabetical list of festivals, p. 115](https://dspace.gipe.ac.in/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10973/39692/GIPE-043972.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=3) gives short English definitions for Unmilani and Trispr̥śā. It is secondary and compressed.
