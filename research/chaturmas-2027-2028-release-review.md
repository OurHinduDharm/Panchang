# Chaturmas 2027–2028 release review — 27 November 2026

Apply the existing 2026/2029/2030 product convention, not a new universal
Chaturmas rule: Smarta Devashayani Ekadashi starts the period, sunrise
Dwadashi transitions for the middle three dietary phases, Smarta Devutthana
Ekadashi ends the period and the subsequent sunrise Kartika Shukla Dwadashi
is the completion observance. Keep the existing public copy and schema.

| Year | Start | Shak → dahi | Dahi → doodh | Doodh → dal | Smarta end | Completion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2027 | Jul 14 | Aug 13 | Sep 12 | Oct 12 | Nov 10 | Nov 11 |
| 2028 | Jul 2 | Aug 2 | Aug 31 | Sep 30 | Oct 28 | Oct 30 |

`data/references/ohd-dwadashi-2027-review.json` and
