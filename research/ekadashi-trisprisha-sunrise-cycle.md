# Ekadashi / Trisprisha sunrise-cycle review

Part of #546 and #369. Independent local tithi evidence; not a publication or fasting instruction.

* Dharmasindhu first pariccheda, Smarta decision: https://www.transliteral.org/pages/z80421015418/view explicitly distinguishes sunrise-vedha for Smartas and arunodaya-vedha for Vaishnavas. Its rule for Ekadashi-only increase distinguishes householders (earlier) and yatis (later). Full examples in https://whm.cdsharma.in/dharma-nirnay?g=ds&p=3&t=path include viddha Ekadashi with Dwadashi loss.
* Nirnayasindhu first pariccheda, Ekadashi decision: https://whm.cdsharma.in/dharma-nirnay?g=ns&p=5&t=path discusses viddha-adhika Ekadashi / nyuna Dwadashi; householders' earlier fast vs later observance for mumukshu etc., and cites Kurma, Vrddha Shatatapa and Skanda. Nakta and purpose/adhikara distinctions must not be collapsed into a universal recommendation.
* Drik https://www.drikpanchang.com/dwadashi/mahadwadashi/trisparsha-mahadwadashi.html?lang=hi describes the sunrise Ekadashi, intervening Dwadashi, next sunrise Trayodashi pattern and combined/two-fast alternatives. Delhi May 2030 https://www.drikpanchang.com/panchang/month-panchang.html?date=20%2F05%2F2030&lang=en lists Mohini 13 May, Gauna/Vaishnava 14 May. This is independent comparative evidence, not a Pithoragarh calculation input.
* Existing #369 reference records already separately review Madhava/Parashurama Dwadashi on 14 May. Those are not equivalent to an Ekadashi date rule. Siddhilakshmi requires its own source.

The new scan checks consecutive actual sunrise contexts, unique sunrise tithi, exact continuous Dasami/Ekadashi/Dwadashi/Trayodashi interval transitions, and conflicting data. It derives candidates per pattern rather than from historical Gregorian dates. At both pakshas it records the earlier/later candidate distinction and arunodaya evidence, but never returns a final date or a parana window. Other Mahadvadashi types, differences of Smarta tradition, eligibility, nakta and special parana exceptions require separate review. Annual review artifacts for 2030/2031 now include the scan; public data and shared sunrise selector unchanged.

## Annual scanner correction and shared engine integration
The actual annual export contains exactly 60,000 ms between successive tithi intervals because the pinned scanner resumes one minute after a boundary (existing ohdNormalizeTransitionIntervals in classical-yogas.js documents this). Real 2030 data: May 13 Dasami ends 03:03:13.733Z, next interval begins 03:04:13.733Z. Normalize only that exact gap between consecutive tithi indices inside each known annual partition; keep original ends, never mutate raw data or accept arbitrary gaps. The shared annual engine now detects this specific exception before generic sunrise selection. An explicit ekadashiTradition: "smarta-householder" selects the earlier candidate only if the full cycle is uniquely scoped and the earlier day is among the matching annual contexts. Unspecified tradition or incomplete/conflicting cycle evidence remains manual review; no automatic approval or special parana. Dharmasindhu first pariccheda examples https://whm.cdsharma.in/dharma-nirnay?g=ds&p=3&t=path and concise Smarta rule https://www.transliteral.org/pages/z80421015418/view specifically distinguish Ekadashi-only vriddhi for householders and yatis. Nirnayasindhu https://whm.cdsharma.in/dharma-nirnay?g=ns&p=5&t=path additionally discusses nakta and purpose-specific variants. The explicit householder candidate is not a universal instruction; this does not alter public cards or assume owner approval of a default tradition.

## Parana source-window review
Dharmasindhu first pariccheda, Parana-nirnaya, https://whm.cdsharma.in/dharma-nirnay?g=ds&p=4&t=path distinguishes the first quarter of Dwadashi (Hari Vasara), Trayodashi parana if Dwadashi is wholly absent, and divergent morning vs Aparahna opinions. Nirnayasindhu first pariccheda https://whm.cdsharma.in/dharma-nirnay?g=ns&p=6&t=path cites Vishnudharma for Hari Vasara and records the morning-three-muhurta and Aparahna disagreement. Its preceding section https://whm.cdsharma.in/dharma-nirnay?g=ns&p=5&t=path additionally distinguishes the householder nakta and purpose-based fast rules. Viramitrodaya Samayaprakasha https://vishvasa.github.io/kalpAntaram/dharmaH/nibandhaH/vIra-mitrodayaH/samaya-prakAshaH/ lists Mahadvadashi variants. Contemporary comparison: Drik calendar https://www.drikpanchang.com/panchang/month-panchang.html?date=20%2F05%2F2030&lang=en and Ahmedabad https://www.shubhpanchang.in/festivals/mohini-ekadashi-2030?lang=en (different city, comparison only). The new shared helper returns separate geometrical morning, fourth-fifth Aparahna and broader post-Madhyahna daylight intersections after Hari Vasara for the earlier fast; for later fast, it validates Trayodashi at the next sunrise and computes a diagnostic morning intersection. No window is automatically approved, no date hard-coded, no public parana or festival content changed. Local sunset bounds are intentionally enforced for the diagnostic windows; the source-specific choice of endpoint and nakta/eligibility remain unresolved.
