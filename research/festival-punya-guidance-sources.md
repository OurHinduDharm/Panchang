# Festival devotional guidance (2026-11-27)

Editorial guidance only. No observance dates, rules, links or existing descriptions are replaced.

- Manvadi/Yugadi: Matsya Purana chapter 17, particularly 4–10, describes these as meritorious for ancestral shraddha. Sanskrit: https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/मत्स्यपुराणम्/अध्यायः_१७ . Tarpana and donations are qualified by family tradition and ritual eligibility; no universal pinda rule is asserted.
- Sankranti: Matsya Purana chapter 99 describes a Sankranti observance with bathing and worship of Surya; chapter 274 includes Sankranti among occasions for dana. Nirnayasindhu Sankranti-nirnaya discusses the appropriate punyakala for ritual observances. For a secondary discussion of snana/dana/japa/shraddha/homa see https://www.transliteral.org/pages/z20102014206/view .
- Stotra recitation to a devotee's chosen deity is a general optional devotional suggestion, not asserted to be a specific mandatory Sankranti injunction or a substitute for prescribed rites. Mahapata guidance already contains this suggestion and is unchanged.
- Manvadi/Yugadi publication dates and month/name variants are outside this PR and remain subject to #519 review. Existing approved cards only receive the general guidance; nothing new is approved.
