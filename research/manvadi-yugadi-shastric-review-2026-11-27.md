# Independent Manvadi/Yugadi textual review — 2026-11-27

Research only; PR #519. Preserve the Taraprasad transcription unchanged. The original page 15 was not re-inspected. This review supplies independent textual evidence, not publication approval.

## Sources
- Matsya Purana 17.4–10: https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/मत्स्यपुराणम्/अध्यायः_१७ — lists four Yugadi and fourteen Manvantara tithis, and their merit for ancestral rites. In 17.7 the Amavasya is Phalguna, unlike the Shravana Amavasya of the printed list; do not silently reconcile this. 17.4 does not expressly resolve the Magha fifteenth's paksha.
- Nirnayasindhu, second pariccheda, Chaitra (Manvadi decision) and Vaishakha (Yugadi decision): https://vishvasa.github.io/kalpAntaram/dharmaH/nibandhaH/kamalAkarabhaTTaH/nirNayasindhuH/ — search for एताश्च मन्वादयो and इयं युगादिरपि. Cites Garuda/Padma, Hemadri and Dipika for Shukla forenoon and Krishna afternoon, for both deva and pitri rites. Cites Smrityarthasara's Shukla sunrise rule as a variant. Explicitly rejects applying afternoon uniformly; defines Yugadi purvahna as the first half of daylight (द्वेधाभक्तदिनपूर्वार्धः). For Akshaya Tritiya, if both days cover purvahna the later day is selected. Do not generalize all tie cases without checking.
- Nirnayasindhu cites Ratnamala for Magha Krishna 15, Bhadrapada Krishna 13, Vaishakha Shukla 3, Kartika Shukla 9. Discusses full-moon versus new-moon variant and differing yuga/kalpa traditions. Also cites sources prescribing Manvadi and Yugadi shraddha in both months when adhika occurs, and pin da-less rites. Review exact ritual wording and all tie/fallback cases before implementation.
- Dharmasindhu Marathi rendering (cross-check, not a substitute for Sanskrit): https://www.transliteral.org/pages/z80422070211/view and https://www.transliteral.org/pages/z80422071006/view — Krishna Manvadi afternoon, fourth of five daylight divisions; Yugadi chapter includes both-month rule for adhika Vaishakha. Clarify whether this fivefold afternoon applies under chosen Nirnayasindhu convention, which explicitly defines a half-day window in its Yugadi discussion.
- Drik Manvadi name list: https://www.drikpanchang.com/tutorials/tithis/manvadi-tithis.html and dated calendar https://www.drikpanchang.com/panchang/tithi/manvadi/manvadi-days.html?year=2026 — corroborates all 14 printed month/paksha/tithi/name pairs up to spelling variants. The dated New Delhi listing explicitly calls the September 4 and 10 Krishna entries Bhadrapada while its tutorial calls them Shravana. This supports interpreting the source's Krishna month labels as amanta, then advancing one lunar month for Purnimanta display, not changing the tithi.
- Drik Yugadi dated calendar https://www.drikpanchang.com/panchang/tithi/yugadi/yugadi-days.html — same four tithi positions after amanta-to-purnimanta mapping, but different yuga-name assignments. Not a primary source.
- Central Sanskrit University study material https://sanskrit.nic.in/msp/upload/digitized_slm_pdf/Acharya_Bridge/Jyotisham/FJAB_01_Patha_13_16.pdf, printed p.50, section 16.7: extracted text of Muhurtachintamani verse corroborates fourteen and four tithi positions (including Shravana Krishna Ashtami/Amavasya), and section 16.7.1 discusses alternate Narada Purana yuga associations. The page image request failed; treat as supplementary textual evidence.

## Normalization candidate (Purnimanta)

The printed table's two Krishna Manvadi rows align with dated Drik evidence, not Matsya's Phalguna Amavasya variant. Apply conversion only once, and account for intercalary month identity in calendar code.
