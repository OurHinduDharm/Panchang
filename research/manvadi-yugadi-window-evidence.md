## Shukla Manvadi shraddha in the shared annual calculation

Dharmasindhu, second pariccheda, Chaitra, Manvadi-nirnaya defines the ordinary ritual period as “पूर्वाह्णोत्रद्वेधाविभक्तदिनपूर्वोभागः” (spacing normalized). It lists twelve Shukla month/tithi pairs. Individual Manu names follow the project's printed catalogue. [Sanskrit text, Chaitra](https://whm.cdsharma.in/dharma-nirnay?g=ds&p=8&t=path)

Nirnayasindhu records Hemadri's Garuda citation “पूर्वाह्णे तु सदा ग्राह्याः शुक्ला मनुयुगादयः” and the alternative Kaladarsha reading. It follows the paksha-specific reading and cites Smritichandrika for performing shraddha in both months of an intercalation. This calculation therefore keeps both cycles. An individual's inability to perform the morning rite cannot be inferred from an ephemeris. [Second pariccheda, Tithi-nirnaya](https://www.transliteral.org/pages/z130524060138/view)

Each occurrence uses positive overlap with the first half of local daylight. Two morning matches, missing coverage, an incomplete year boundary or conflicting month evidence leave the date open. The regular and intercalary cycles can differ from the modern festival list. For example the Delhi 2031 list has the regular Bhadrapada Tritiya on September 19. The extra Bhadrapada shraddha is independently required under the cited two-month provision. The Krishna Amavasya disagreement and Yugadi naming remain in the source review above. [Delhi 2031 comparison](https://www.drikpanchang.com/panchang/tithi/manvadi/manvadi-days.html?lang=en&year=2031)

### Pithoragarh, Chaitra 2028

The archived March 28 card labelled its tithi Tritiya but called it Dvitiya and Yugadi in the description. The annual tithi transition on March 28 is 13:03 IST, after the local daylight midpoint (about 12:14 IST). Tritiya covers the March 29 forenoon. The archive correction applies the site's Manvadi **shraddha** convention. The Delhi Drik calendar lists March 28; its displayed festival date is retained only as a comparison.
