## Important caveat

The table above deliberately preserves the source's event labels and timestamps instead of pretending every Drik “Asta starts/ends” boundary is automatically identical to each Ganesh Martand “पूर्व/पश्चिम अस्त/उदय” entry. The Drik result pages are location-specific, and 24+ hour times must be normalized to the next civil date. A proper exact-time comparison needs a single declared location, local timezone, sunrise-day assignment, and a verified mapping of eastern/western setting/rising to each published boundary.

## Initial conclusion

- Several Mercury cycle dates align closely or fall within a small number of days, especially the July, August/September, October/November and January cycles.
- Some events differ by one or more dates, and several listed clock times cannot yet be compared directly because the event direction/phase mapping is not proven.
- This supports using Ganesh Martand as a valuable traditional benchmark, while demonstrating why the research must keep source method and event identity explicit.
- Do not use these preliminary comparisons to alter production calculations or declare all Mercury events validated.
