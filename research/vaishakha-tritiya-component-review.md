## 28 November follow-up: Parashurama first-yama vs Pradosha

Dharmasindhu Sanskrit, second pariccheda Vaishakha, printed-text transcription: https://whm.cdsharma.in/dharma-nirnay?g=ds&p=9&t=path . It specifies first-night-yama coverage for the date, first day if only its first yama is covered, later if both have coverage, and Pradosha for puja/arghya. Compare the Marathi rendering https://www.transliteral.org/pages/z80422071133/view. Nirnayasindhu second pariccheda https://www.transliteral.org/pages/z130524062437/view quotes Bhargavarchanadipika and Bhavishya for first-yama and has an earlier-day fallback phrasing whose scope must be reconciled. The two texts must not be treated as an identical universal fallback rule.

The old three-night-muhurta resolver produced 4 May 2030 although Tritiya covered only the next evening. The new offline component evidence explicitly calculates quarter-night and three-night-muhurta coverage, with complete interval and continuity guards. A sole covered later first-yama gives a 5 May diagnostic date, consistent with Drik's New Delhi calendar https://www.drikpanchang.com/calendars/hindu/hinducalendar.html?lang=sa&year=2030 (not an input). Disagreement with the old resolver blocks the component candidate instead of treating 4 May as established. No final choice of fallback or published date is implied; shared rule resolver and public calendar are unchanged. A subsequent rule correction requires the exact source interpretation, other cases, and regression review.
